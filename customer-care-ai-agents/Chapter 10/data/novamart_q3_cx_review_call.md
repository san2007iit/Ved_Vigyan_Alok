# NovaMart — Q3 2026 Customer Experience Review Call (Excerpt)

**Date:** October 22, 2026
**Participants:** Sunita Menon (Chief Customer Officer), Kavita Rao (Head of Contact
Centres), and business-unit heads.

---

**Moderator:** Good afternoon, and welcome to the third-quarter customer-experience review.
Over to Sunita.

**Sunita Menon (CCO):** Thank you. Volumes were high: we handled **1.38 million contacts**
in Q3, up 16% year over year, ahead of the festive season. The good news is self-service:
it now resolves **36%** of issues, an all-time high. CSAT for the quarter was **4.1**.

**Kavita Rao (Head of Contact Centres):** A few service metrics. I want to flag that our
**first-contact resolution dipped to 68% at the end of Q3**, down from 71% at the start
of the year. Two causes: the FrostLine relay back-order, which forces a second visit on
many refrigerator repairs, and the festive surge, which brought in many new agents. Our
average first response on enterprise priority tickets was **3.7 hours** in Q3, still
inside the 4-hour SLA but closer to it than we would like. We expect FCR to recover
**above 70% by the end of Q4** once relay stock arrives on 15 November.

---

### Q&A

**Arvind Rao (Head of B2B Accounts):** Kavita, what does the response-time trend mean for
our enterprise contracts?

**Kavita Rao:** The SLA that matters is the **4-hour first response** on priority tickets.
At 3.7 hours we are within the SLA, so no service credits are due, but the headroom is
thin. FCR is not a contractual metric with our B2B customers.

**Neha Kapoor (Customer Success):** Are we still on track to launch WhatsApp support in Q4?

**Sunita Menon:** That is the plan, subject to the service-quality policy review. We have
trained 140 agents for the channel.

**Arvind Rao:** And CallBridge? Any change in their share of voice volume?

**Kavita Rao:** CallBridge still handles about **45% of voice contacts**. We have started
onboarding a second partner, but it will not take meaningful volume before Q1.

**Moderator:** That concludes today's review. Thank you.
