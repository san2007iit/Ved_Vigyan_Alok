# NovaMart — Customer Service Quality Policy (Excerpt)

> *Synthetic internal policy created for the Agentic RAG lab. NovaMart is a fictional
> retailer; thresholds are illustrative.*

**Policy ID:** CX-POL-007
**Owner:** Office of the Chief Customer Officer
**Last reviewed:** March 2026

## 1. Purpose

This policy sets the minimum service-quality standards that the customer-care organisation
must meet before NovaMart **launches a new support channel** (for example WhatsApp or video
support) or **moves additional volume to an outsourcing partner**. It applies to all
consumer and enterprise support operations.

## 2. Service-quality thresholds

Using the **most recently reported figures** (the latest quarterly review if it is more
recent than the annual report), the CX analyst must verify that:

- **First-contact resolution (FCR) ≥ 70%.** FCR between **65% and 70%** is **amber**: a
  launch may proceed only with a documented remediation plan approved by the CX Committee.
  FCR **below 65%** is a **hard block**: no new channel may launch, regardless of other factors.
- **CSAT ≥ 4.0** (on a 1–5 scale). Below 4.0 requires a CX Committee exception.
- **Repeat-contact rate (7 days) ≤ 20%.**

## 3. SLA awareness

Where NovaMart has contractual service levels with enterprise customers, the analyst must
record each SLA threshold and confirm the current headroom. Operating **within 10% of an SLA
threshold** (for a 4-hour first-response SLA, at or above 3.6 hours) must be flagged in the
launch memo even if the policy thresholds above are met.

## 4. Vendor concentration

No single outsourcing partner may handle more than **50% of any channel's volume**. A partner
above **40%** is flagged as **elevated vendor risk**, and new volume may not be moved to that
partner until a second partner is live.

## 5. Documentation

Every launch memo must cite the specific figures used to evaluate Sections 2–4 and name the
source document and reporting period. Unsupported assertions are not acceptable; if a required
figure cannot be found, the analyst must say so explicitly rather than estimate.
