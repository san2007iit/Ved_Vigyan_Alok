# NovaMart Retail Pvt. Ltd. — Annual Customer Experience Report 2025 (Excerpt)

**Year ended December 31, 2025**

> *This is a synthetic, illustrative excerpt created for the Agentic RAG lab.
> NovaMart is a fictional retailer. None of the figures below are real.*

## 1. Business overview

NovaMart sells consumer electronics and home appliances (smartphones, televisions,
refrigerators, air conditioners, water purifiers, kitchen appliances) online and through
212 stores across India. Customer care runs on four channels: **voice**, **chat**,
**email**, and the **in-app help centre** (self-service). Field service (installation and
repair visits) is delivered by NovaMart technicians and three authorised partners.

## 2. Customer-experience risks (selected)

- **Vendor concentration.** One outsourcing partner, CallBridge Services, handled **44%
  of all voice contacts** in 2025. A disruption at CallBridge would materially affect
  service levels.
- **Spare parts.** The cooling relay used in FrostLine refrigerators is sourced from a
  single supplier. A supply disruption would delay repairs and raise repeat contacts.
- **Seasonality.** Contact volumes during the October–November festive season run at
  roughly **1.4x** the monthly average.
- **Enterprise SLAs.** Our B2B service contracts commit to a **first response within 4
  hours** for priority tickets. Repeated breaches allow customers to claim service credits.

## 3. Performance in 2025

NovaMart handled **4.82 million customer contacts** in 2025, up 14% from 4.23 million in
2024, while orders grew 19%. Contacts per 100 orders therefore fell to **38.5** from 40.2.
The share of issues resolved through **self-service** rose to **31%** from 24%, driven by
the new in-app order-tracking and installation-booking flows.

Average **CSAT** was **4.3 / 5** (2024: 4.1) and **Net Promoter Score** was **41** (2024: 36).
Cost per assisted contact fell to **Rs 86** from Rs 94.

### Service-quality scorecard (year-end figures)

| Metric                                   | 2025  | 2024  |
|------------------------------------------|------:|------:|
| First-contact resolution (FCR)           | 71%   | 67%   |
| Repeat-contact rate (7 days)             | 17%   | 21%   |
| Average first response, enterprise (h)   | 3.2   | 4.6   |
| CSAT (1–5)                               | 4.3   | 4.1   |
| Self-service resolution share            | 31%   | 24%   |

**First-contact resolution** at year-end 2025 was **71%** (issues resolved in the first
contact ÷ all issues), up from 67%, comfortably above the internal 70% floor. Average
first response for enterprise priority tickets was **3.2 hours**, inside the 4-hour
contractual SLA.

## 4. Outlook for 2026

Management expects contact volumes of **5.3 to 5.5 million** in 2026, with self-service
growing faster than assisted channels. We plan to launch a **WhatsApp support channel**
in 2026 and to invest approximately **Rs 18 crore** in agent training and a new
knowledge base.
