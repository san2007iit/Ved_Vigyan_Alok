# Part 2: The Gāyatrī (Sāvitrī) Mantra, 15 word-by-word interpretations

> **ॐ भूर्भुवः स्वः। तत्सवितुर्वरेण्यं भर्गो देवस्य धीमहि। धियो यो नः प्रचोदयात्॥**
>
> *om bhūr bhuvaḥ svaḥ | tat savitur vareṇyaṃ bhargo devasya dhīmahi | dhiyo yo naḥ pracodayāt ||*

### Grammatical observations

1. **The gender crux.** तत्, वरेण्यम्, भर्गः are all **neuter accusative singular**. यः is **masculine nominative**. So "who impels" can only be **Savitā deva** (masc.), not *bharga* (neut.). The grammar separates the **source** (Savitṛ, a person, the *śaktimān*) from its **emanation** (bharga, an energy, the *śakti*). We meditate on the energy; the person does the impelling.
2. **Person structure (Nirukta 7.1–7.2 again).** धीमहि is **1st person** (an ādhyātmika feature). प्रचोदयात् is **3rd person** (a parokṣakṛta feature). There is **no 2nd person**. The Gāyatrī is the grammatical *complement* of Part 1's mantra (all 2nd person, pratyakṣakṛta): there the deity is addressed directly; here the deity is spoken *about*, while the speakers name their own act.
3. **Two moods, two agents.** धीमहि (लिङ्, we) and प्रचोदयात् (लेट्, he) are both *non-indicative*, expressing potential or aspiration. The mantra is a **conditional coupling**: our holding-in-mind and his impelling.
4. **Ambiguity of धियः.** The form is द्वितीया बहुवचन (standard: "our intellects"), but it is also षष्ठी/पञ्चमी एकवचन. Several interpretations below use this (e.g. "from the intellect").
5. **Two derivations of धीमहि.** √ध्यै gives "meditate" (Sāyaṇa: *ध्यायाम*). √धा gives "hold, place, receive" (several modern Vedicists read it as a root-aorist optative of √dhā). Both are kept below as अनन्त अर्थ.

## 4. Literal base translation

> "Om. Earth, mid-space, heaven. May we hold in mind (or receive) that choice-worthy radiance of the divine Impeller, who shall impel our intellects (and actions) forward."

## 5. Part 1 and Part 2 as one operator

| Function | Part 1 (YV 30.3) | Part 2 (YV 30.2) |
|---|---|---|
| Source | देव सवितः (vocative) | सवितुः देवस्य (genitive) |
| Removal vector | **परा सुव** दुरितानि | **भर्ग** (√भ्रस्ज, "burning out" faults) |
| Supply vector | **आ सुव** भद्रम् | **प्रचोदयात्** धियः |
| Beneficiary | नः | नः |
| Grammatical person | 2nd (pratyakṣakṛta) | 1st + 3rd (ādhyātmika + parokṣakṛta) |
| Domain of the good | भद्र (whatever is wholesome) | धी (the capacity that recognises the wholesome) |

**Synthesis.** Part 1 asks Savitā to *sort the world* (expel durita, bring bhadra). The Gāyatrī asks Savitā to *upgrade the sorter* (impel dhī). Read together, the two mantras describe a complete cybernetic system: **regulate the environment, and improve the regulator.**

# The 15 interpretations

Each interpretation keeps the **same padaccheda and the same grammatical frame** (§2). Only the domain of each word's value changes, which is the principle of *ananta artha* as validated in Part 1 §12.2.

## Interpretation 1: Vyākaraṇa-based (the grammarian's reading)

**Padaccheda:** ॐ । भूः । भुवः । स्वः । तत् । सवितुः । वरेण्यम् । भर्गः । देवस्य । धीमहि । धियः । यः । नः । प्रचोदयात् ॥

**Premise.** Meaning is built strictly from morphology: root + suffix + case + mood. Nothing is imported.

| पद | Grammatical anchor | Meaning in this reading |
|---|---|---|
| ॐ | √अव् + मन्, अव्यय | "Protective one": a sentence-external invocation marker |
| भूः | √भू + क्विप् | That which *is* (bare existence) |
| भुवः | √भू (Vedic stem) | That which *becomes* |
| स्वः | स्वर् (अव्यय) | That which *shines* |
| तत् | द्वितीया, नपुं. | That (the object) |
| सवितुः | √सू + तृच् (agent), षष्ठी | Of the *agent of impelling* (tṛc denotes a habitual or professional agent) |
| वरेण्यम् | √वृ + एण्य (gerundive) | Which *ought to be* chosen |
| भर्गः | √भ्रस्ज + असुन् | The *roasting / maturing* brilliance |
| देवस्य | √दिव् + अच्, षष्ठी | Of the radiant one |
| धीमहि | लिङ्, आ. उत्तम बहु. | *May we* hold / meditate (optative, collective, ātmanepada = for ourselves) |
| धियः | द्वि. बहु. | Thought-acts (plural) |
| यः | पुं. प्रथमा | He who (refers to Savitṛ) |
| नः | ष. बहु. | Of us |
| प्रचोदयात् | प्र + √चुद + णिच् + लेट् | *May he cause* to move forward (causative + subjunctive) |

**Full interpretation.** "Protector! Being, becoming, shining. May we, for our own sake, hold in mind that brilliance, the maturing, choice-worthy brilliance, belonging to the habitual Impeller, the Radiant, who is to cause our many thought-acts to move forward."

**Key insight.** The **causative (णिच्)** in प्रचोदयात् is decisive: Savitṛ does not think *for* us; he *causes our thoughts to move*. Agency is shared. The **ātmanepada** of धीमहि marks the meditation as benefiting the meditators, while the **tṛc** suffix on सवितृ marks impelling as Savitṛ's *constant nature*, not a one-off act.

## Interpretation 2: Nirukta-based (Yāska's reading)

**Padaccheda:** ॐ । भूः । भुवः । स्वः । तत् । सवितुः । वरेण्यम् । भर्गः । देवस्य । धीमहि । धियः । यः । नः । प्रचोदयात् ॥

**Premise.** Each word is opened through *all* attested derivations (…इति वा …इति वा), and they are layered.

| पद | Nirukta derivation(s) | Meaning in this reading |
|---|---|---|
| ॐ | अवति (protects, knows, pervades) | The protecting-knowing-pervading one |
| भूः | भवति | The domain of manifestation (earth, fire, ṛk, prāṇa) |
| भुवः | भावयति (cause to be) | The domain that sustains becoming (mid-space, air, sāman, apāna) |
| स्वः | सु-अरण (good movement) / स्वयं राजते | The domain of self-shining (heaven, sun, yajus, vyāna) |
| तत् | — | That (the transcendent referent) |
| सवितुः | सर्वस्य प्रसविता (Nir. 10.31) | Of the universal generator |
| वरेण्यम् | वरणीय | Fit to be chosen, as a bride chooses (√वृ, cf. *svayaṃvara*) |
| भर्गः | भासयति + रञ्जयति + गच्छति (Maitrī 6.7) / भर्जयति | Illumining-delighting-moving, *and* burning-purifying light |
| देवस्य | दानात् दीपनात् द्योतनात् द्युस्थानात् | Of the one who gives, kindles, shines, and dwells in light |
| धीमहि | ध्यायेम / धारयेम | Let us meditate on *and* bear |
| धियः | प्रज्ञाः *and* कर्माणि (Nighaṇṭu 3.9; 2.1) | Insights and actions |
| यः | — | Who |
| नः | — | Ours |
| प्रचोदयात् | प्रकर्षेण चोदयेत् | Impel excellently |

**Full interpretation.** "O Protector, pervading earth, mid-space and heaven: let us both meditate on and carry within us that choice-worthy light of the all-generating Giver, a light that illumines, delights and moves us while also burning away our impurities, so that he may excellently impel our insights *and* our deeds."

**Key insight.** Because the Nighaṇṭu lists **धी** under both *prajñā* and *karma*, the Gāyatrī is not only a prayer for intelligence. It asks for the **integration of knowing and doing** (*jñāna-karma-samuccaya*), and Yāska's lexicon makes that reading grammatically legitimate.

## Interpretation 3: Cosmological model

**Padaccheda:** ॐ । भूः । भुवः । स्वः । तत् । सवितुः । वरेण्यम् । भर्गः । देवस्य । धीमहि । धियः । यः । नः । प्रचोदयात् ॥

**Premise.** Savitṛ is the cosmic generator, a star and stellar process. The mantra describes the energy chain from star to planet to complexity.

| पद | Cosmological value |
|---|---|
| ॐ | The primordial cosmic impulse (*śabda* as the first vibration; compare the acoustic oscillations of the early-universe plasma, preserved in the CMB) |
| भूः | Condensed planetary matter (pṛthivī-loka) |
| भुवः | The interplanetary / interstellar medium (antarikṣa): plasma, solar wind, magnetic fields |
| स्वः | The luminous stellar domain (dyu-loka) |
| तत् | That remote source |
| सवितुः | Of the generating star |
| वरेण्यम् | The "selected" parameter regime: a stable main-sequence star in a habitable configuration |
| भर्गः | Radiant energy from **nucleosynthesis**: √भ्रस्ज "to roast" = the stellar furnace in which elements are cooked |
| देवस्य | Of the luminous body |
| धीमहि | We (the planetary system) **receive and store** (√धा): photosynthetic fixation, fossil and biomass storage |
| धियः | Ordered, information-bearing structures: chemical, biological and eventually cognitive complexity |
| यः | Which star |
| नः | Our planetary system |
| प्रचोदयात् | Drives forward: energy flow powers the rise of complexity |

**Full interpretation.** "Across the three tiers of a star–planet system (dense planet, intervening medium, radiant star), we receive and hold the choice-worthy energy of that generating star, energy forged in its nuclear furnace. That star drives our ordered structures forward into ever greater complexity."

**Key insight.** Astrophysicist Eric Chaisson's measure, *energy-rate density* (energy flow per unit mass), rises from stars to planets to plants to brains to societies. In this model the Gāyatrī's **bharga → dhīmahi → dhiyaḥ** chain is exactly that gradient: stellar energy, captured and stored, raising complexity up to *dhī*. The root √भ्रस्ज ("to cook") is a strong fit for nucleosynthesis, since the carbon and oxygen in a meditator's brain were "cooked" in earlier stars.

## Interpretation 4: Wave mechanics / vibration model

**Padaccheda:** ॐ । भूः । भुवः । स्वः । तत् । सवितुः । वरेण्यम् । भर्गः । देवस्य । धीमहि । धियः । यः । नः । प्रचोदयात् ॥

**Premise.** Reality as oscillation; the mantra as a description of **driven resonance**.

| पद | Wave-mechanical value |
|---|---|
| ॐ | Fundamental frequency / carrier wave. Phonetically A‑U‑M sweeps the whole vocal tract (throat → palate → lips → nasal closure), so it is a "full-band" sound |
| भूः | Low-frequency, high-amplitude band (dense, slow) |
| भुवः | Middle band (transitional) |
| स्वः | High-frequency band (subtle, luminous: electromagnetic) |
| तत् | That emitter |
| सवितुः | Of the oscillator, the driving source |
| वरेण्यम् | The **selected resonant mode** (√वृ "choose": boundary conditions choose the eigenmodes) |
| भर्गः | Radiated intensity, the energy carried by the wave |
| देवस्य | Of the radiator |
| धीमहि | We **tune / phase-lock** to it (meditation as matching one's resonance) |
| धियः | Our internal oscillators (neural, respiratory and cardiac rhythms) |
| यः | Which source |
| नः | Ours |
| प्रचोदयात् | **Drives / entrains** them (a driven oscillator) |

**Full interpretation.** "Across all bands, low, middle and high, we tune ourselves to the chosen resonant mode of the radiating source, so that it may entrain and drive our own internal oscillations."

**Key insight.** A driven oscillator absorbs energy efficiently only **at resonance**. Grammatically, **dhīmahi** (our tuning) comes *before* **pracodayāt** (his driving), which is the physically necessary order. There is empirical support for physiological entrainment: Bernardi et al. (*BMJ*, 2001) found that recited mantras and rosary prayer slowed breathing to about 6 breaths per minute and synchronised cardiovascular rhythms (baroreflex resonance). Metrically regular recitation such as the 3 × 8 Gāyatrī is a plausible candidate for this effect.

## Interpretation 5: Quantum physics analogy

**Padaccheda:** ॐ । भूः । भुवः । स्वः । तत् । सवितुः । वरेण्यम् । भर्गः । देवस्य । धीमहि । धियः । यः । नः । प्रचोदयात् ॥

**Premise.** An analogy only. Savitṛ as the **generator of dynamics**.

| पद | Quantum analogue |
|---|---|
| ॐ | The quantum vacuum / ground state: not empty, full of potential |
| भूः | Actualised states (definite outcomes, the "particle" aspect) |
| भुवः | Unitary becoming (Schrödinger evolution, the "between" of measurements) |
| स्वः | The space of possibilities (Hilbert space, the wave-function) |
| तत् | That (unobserved, beyond classical description) |
| सवितुः | Of the **Hamiltonian, the generator** of time evolution (Stone's theorem: U(t) = e^(−iHt/ħ)) |
| वरेण्यम् | **Selection** (√वृ "choose"): the outcome picked out in measurement / einselection (decoherence-selected states) |
| भर्गः | Energy quanta (E = hν), the radiant content |
| देवस्य | Of the field |
| धीमहि | We register / observe (entanglement of the apparatus with the system) |
| धियः | The observer's record states |
| यः | Which generator |
| नः | Ours |
| प्रचोदयात् | Evolves our states forward |

**Full interpretation.** "Through actuality, becoming and possibility, we register the selected energy-content of that Generator of evolution, which carries our own record-states forward in time."

**Key insight.** The closest mapping in this whole document is **सविता = प्रसविता = "generator"**. In quantum mechanics the Hamiltonian is technically called the *generator* of time translations, and the Sanskrit agent noun *sa-vi-tṛ* ("the one who impels forth") names that role exactly. *Caveat:* claims that consciousness causes collapse are contested and are **not** asserted here; "dhīmahi" maps onto registration or measurement in general.

## Interpretation 6: Thermodynamics / energy transformation

**Padaccheda:** ॐ । भूः । भुवः । स्वः । तत् । सवितुः । वरेण्यम् । भर्गः । देवस्य । धीमहि । धियः । यः । नः । प्रचोदयात् ॥

**Premise.** The mantra as a description of energy *quality* and its conversion into ordered work.

| पद | Thermodynamic value |
|---|---|
| ॐ | Total conserved energy (first law, the invariant) |
| भूः | Stored / potential energy (chemical bonds, matter) |
| भुवः | Transitional energy (heat flow, work, kinetic) |
| स्वः | Radiant energy |
| तत् | That source |
| सवितुः | Of the **low-entropy source** (the Sun's ~5,800 K photons) |
| वरेण्यम् | **Exergy**: the *usable*, choice-worthy fraction of energy |
| भर्गः | The transforming heat, √भ्रस्ज *pāka* = cooking, metabolism, chemical conversion |
| देवस्य | Of the giver (dāna) of free energy |
| धीमहि | We **capture and store** it (√धा): photosynthesis → glucose → ATP |
| धियः | Ordered structures, capacities to do work |
| यः | Which source |
| नः | Ours |
| प्रचोदयात् | **Drives** dissipative structures far from equilibrium (Prigogine) |

**Full interpretation.** "Across stored, flowing and radiant energy, we capture the high-quality, transforming energy of that low-entropy Giver, which keeps our ordered structures alive, far from equilibrium."

**Key insight.** **वरेण्य = exergy.** The prayer is not for *more* energy (which is conserved anyway) but for *choice-worthy* energy, the low-entropy part that can do work. Schrödinger (*What is Life?*, 1944) said organisms feed on "negative entropy". In thermodynamic terms the Gāyatrī asks for negentropy.

## Interpretation 7: Neuroscience (brain and cognition)

**Padaccheda:** ॐ । भूः । भुवः । स्वः । तत् । सवितुः । वरेण्यम् । भर्गः । देवस्य । धीमहि । धियः । यः । नः । प्रचोदयात् ॥

**Premise.** The mantra as a model of **attention plus motivation** in the brain.

| पद | Neural value |
|---|---|
| ॐ | Baseline resting-state (a coherent global oscillation) |
| भूः | Brainstem and body regulation: homeostasis, "being alive" |
| भुवः | Subcortical-limbic processing: emotion, "moving between" |
| स्वः | Cortical associative processing: the "light" of reflective awareness (*a functional gradient, not MacLean's outdated "triune brain"*) |
| तत् | The attended target |
| सवितुः | Of the **neuromodulatory impeller** (locus coeruleus noradrenaline, VTA dopamine) |
| वरेण्यम् | **Value-selected**: basal-ganglia action selection (√वृ "choose") |
| भर्गः | Metabolic activation energy (the brain uses ~20% of the body's energy) |
| देवस्य | Of the arousing source |
| धीमहि | **Working-memory maintenance** (prefrontal "holding in mind", √धा "hold") |
| धियः | Cognitive representations and networks |
| यः | Which modulator |
| नः | Ours |
| प्रचोदयात् | **Motivational drive** (dopaminergic "incentive salience") |

**Full interpretation.** "At every level of the nervous system, from vital regulation through emotion to reflective cortex, we hold steady in working memory the value-selected, energising signal of the neuromodulatory source, so that it may drive our cognitive networks towards their goals."

**Key insight.** **धीमहि** (holding in mind) and **प्रचोदयात्** (driving forward) correspond to the brain's two main control systems: prefrontal *maintenance* and dopaminergic *motivation*. Meditation research (e.g. Lutz et al., *Trends in Cognitive Sciences*, 2008) describes focused-attention practice as training exactly this ability to sustain attention.

## Interpretation 8: Cognitive science / intelligence model

**Padaccheda:** ॐ । भूः । भुवः । स्वः । तत् । सवितुः । वरेण्यम् । भर्गः । देवस्य । धीमहि । धियः । यः । नः । प्रचोदयात् ॥

**Premise.** Intelligence as **predictive processing**: a generative model that minimises error.

| पद | Cognitive value |
|---|---|
| ॐ | The global workspace: the shared arena of cognition |
| भूः | Sensory data (perceptual grounding) |
| भुवः | Inference (transforming data into hypotheses) |
| स्वः | Abstraction and insight (concepts, "aha") |
| तत् | The target concept |
| सवितुः | Of the **generative model**, the predictor that produces expectations |
| वरेण्यम् | The **optimal hypothesis** (Bayesian posterior: the "chosen" model) |
| भर्गः | The **error-burning signal**: prediction error that "roasts away" wrong beliefs (Mahīdhara: *पापानां तापकम्*) |
| देवस्य | Of the illuminating source |
| धीमहि | We **attend** (precision-weighting: giving the signal weight) |
| धियः | Our mental models |
| यः | Which source |
| नः | Ours |
| प्रचोदयात् | **Updates** (learning: belief revision) |

**Full interpretation.** "From perception through inference to insight, we give attention to the optimal, error-correcting light of the generative source, so that it may update and advance our models of the world."

**Key insight.** Read as cognitive science, the Gāyatrī is a **learning rule**, not a request for answers. It does not ask for any particular piece of knowledge; it asks for the *machinery of knowing* (धी) to be improved. In AI terms, it asks for better learning *algorithms* rather than more stored data.

## Interpretation 9: Consciousness studies

**Padaccheda:** ॐ । भूः । भुवः । स्वः । तत् । सवितुः । वरेण्यम् । भर्गः । देवस्य । धीमहि । धियः । यः । नः । प्रचोदयात् ॥

**Premise.** The mantra as a map of **states of consciousness** and of the awareness–content distinction.

| पद | Consciousness-studies value |
|---|---|
| ॐ | Māṇḍūkya's four: अ waking, उ dream, म deep sleep, silence turīya |
| भूः | Waking (gross objects) |
| भुवः | Dreaming (subtle, internally generated content) |
| स्वः | Deep sleep (contentless rest, the "causal" state) |
| तत् | **Turīya**: pure awareness beyond the three |
| सवितुः | Of the **self-luminous witness** (sākṣin, *svayaṃ-prakāśa*) |
| वरेण्यम् | Most worth identifying with |
| भर्गः | The **luminosity** of awareness, that which makes experience "lit up" |
| देवस्य | Of the self-shining one |
| धीमहि | We **abide** (meta-awareness, dhyāna) |
| धियः | Mental **contents** (vṛttis: thoughts, perceptions) |
| यः | Which witness |
| नः | Ours |
| प्रचोदयात् | Illumines and animates them |

**Full interpretation.** "Through waking, dream and deep sleep we abide in that fourth, the choice-worthy luminosity of the self-shining witness, which lights up and animates all our mental contents."

**Key insight.** The grammar itself separates **भर्गः** (the light: neuter, singular, the object of abiding) from **धियः** (mental states: feminine, plural, the things impelled). This matches the distinction consciousness science draws between the **level or state** of consciousness and its **contents**, as in global-workspace and integrated-information theories. One luminosity, many contents: singular versus plural.

## Interpretation 10: Vedāntic interpretation

**Padaccheda:** ॐ । भूः । भुवः । स्वः । तत् । सवितुः । वरेण्यम् । भर्गः । देवस्य । धीमहि । धियः । यः । नः । प्रचोदयात् ॥

| पद | Vedāntic value |
|---|---|
| ॐ | Brahman as sound (Kaṭha 1.2.16: *एतद्ध्येवाक्षरं ब्रह्म*) |
| भूः | **Sat** (Being) (Dayānanda: *प्राणादपि प्रियः, सर्वेषां सत्ताप्रदः*) |
| भुवः | **Cit** (Consciousness) (Dayānanda: *सर्वदुःखनाशकः*) |
| स्वः | **Ānanda** (Bliss) (Dayānanda: *सुखस्वरूपः सुखप्रदः*) |
| तत् | Brahman: *tat tvam asi* (Chāndogya 6.8.7) |
| सवितुः | Of the antaryāmin, the inner impeller (Bṛhad. 3.7) |
| वरेण्यम् | **Śreyas** as opposed to *preyas*: Kaṭha 1.2.2: *श्रेयो हि धीरोऽभि प्रेयसो वृणीते*, using **the same root √वृ** |
| भर्गः | **Svarūpa-jyoti**: the light that burns avidyā (*jñānāgni*, Gītā 4.37) |
| देवस्य | Of the self-luminous one |
| धीमहि | **Nididhyāsana** (sustained contemplation) |
| धियः | Buddhi-vṛttis, culminating in the *akhaṇḍākāra-vṛtti* (the mental mode that takes the form of the undivided) |
| यः | Who, the Self |
| नः | Of us, jīvas who are ultimately that Self |
| प्रचोदयात् | Illumines the buddhi until it reflects Brahman without distortion |

**Full interpretation.** "Om: Brahman, which is Being, Consciousness and Bliss. We contemplate that supreme light of the indwelling Impeller, the light the wise choose over the pleasant, which burns away ignorance, so that it may illumine our intellects until they reflect the Self as it is."

**Key insight.** In Kaṭha 1.2.2 the *dhīra* (one with steady *dhī*) "*vṛṇīte*" (√वृ) chooses śreyas. The Gāyatrī joins **वरेण्य** (√वृ) and **धी** in the same sentence, the same pair of roots Kaṭha uses for wise choice. The Gāyatrī can therefore be read as a prayer to *become the dhīra who chooses rightly*.

## Interpretation 11: Sāṅkhya interpretation

**Padaccheda:** ॐ । भूः । भुवः । स्वः । तत् । सवितुः । वरेण्यम् । भर्गः । देवस्य । धीमहि । धियः । यः । नः । प्रचोदयात् ॥

| पद | Sāṅkhya value |
|---|---|
| ॐ | The whole of the 25 tattvas: prakṛti together with puruṣa |
| भूः | **Tamas**: the gross elements (mahābhūtas), inertia |
| भुवः | **Rajas**: motion, the senses and their activity |
| स्वः | **Sattva**: illumination, buddhi |
| तत् | **Puruṣa**: beyond the guṇas |
| सवितुः | Of puruṣa, whose mere proximity "impels" prakṛti (Sāṅkhya-kārikā 21, *paṅgv-andha-vat*: like the lame man guiding the blind) |
| वरेण्यम् | What leads to apavarga (release): the goal prakṛti serves (SK 56) |
| भर्गः | The **reflected light** (chāyā) of puruṣa in sattvic buddhi |
| देवस्य | Of the conscious principle |
| धीमहि | Buddhi holds that reflection |
| धियः | **Buddhi (mahat)** and its many modifications |
| यः | Which puruṣa |
| नः | Ours |
| प्रचोदयात् | Sets buddhi moving towards *viveka* (discrimination) |

**Full interpretation.** "Through tamas, rajas and sattva, may our buddhi hold the reflected light of that conscious puruṣa, whose presence alone moves prakṛti, so that this light turns our buddhi towards the discrimination that leads to release."

**Key insight.** Sāṅkhya-kārikā 13 says *सत्त्वं लघु प्रकाशकम्* (sattva is light and illuminating). The **ascending order of the vyāhṛtis (भूः → भुवः → स्वः) follows the guṇa ladder (tamas → rajas → sattva)**, and the rest of the mantra takes place at the sattva level, in buddhi, which is the only faculty clear enough to reflect puruṣa.

## Interpretation 12: Symbolic (light versus ignorance)

**Padaccheda:** ॐ । भूः । भुवः । स्वः । तत् । सवितुः । वरेण्यम् । भर्गः । देवस्य । धीमहि । धियः । यः । नः । प्रचोदयात् ॥

| पद | Symbolic value |
|---|---|
| ॐ | The first sound of dawn: the world waking |
| भूः | Night-bound earth (the darkness of ignorance) |
| भुवः | Twilight, the horizon (doubt, inquiry) |
| स्वः | Daylight, heaven (knowledge) |
| तत् | The Sun still beyond the horizon |
| सवितुः | Of the rising Sun (in the Ṛgveda, Savitṛ is the god who wakes the world and puts it to rest) |
| वरेण्यम् | Longed for by those in darkness |
| भर्गः | The fire that burns darkness away |
| देवस्य | Of the bright one |
| धीमहि | We turn our gaze towards it (as the sunflower turns) |
| धियः | Our inner sight |
| यः | Which Sun |
| नः | Ours |
| प्रचोदयात् | Wakes it up |

**Full interpretation.** "From the darkness of earth through the twilight of the horizon into the light of heaven, we turn towards the longed-for, darkness-burning radiance of the rising Sun, so that it may awaken our inner sight."

**Key insight.** The mantra **acts out a sunrise in its own word order**: it starts at earth (भूः), rises through mid-space (भुवः) to light (स्वः), meets the Sun (सवितुः… भर्गः), then turns the light inward (धियः). It is a prose form of *तमसो मा ज्योतिर्गमय* (Bṛhad. 1.3.28). This is why the Gāyatrī is recited at *sandhyā*, the thresholds of the day.

## Interpretation 13: Information theory

**Padaccheda:** ॐ । भूः । भुवः । स्वः । तत् । सवितुः । वरेण्यम् । भर्गः । देवस्य । धीमहि । धियः । यः । नः । प्रचोदयात् ॥

**Premise.** The mantra as a **communication system**, mapped onto Shannon–Weaver's three levels.

| पद | Information-theoretic value |
|---|---|
| ॐ | **Synchronisation preamble**: the header that opens the channel, as a data packet begins with a sync sequence |
| भूः | **Level A, technical**: is the signal transmitted accurately? (the physical carrier) |
| भुवः | **Level B, semantic**: is the meaning conveyed? |
| स्वः | **Level C, effectiveness**: does the meaning change behaviour? (Weaver, 1949) |
| तत् | The message |
| सवितुः | Of the **information source** |
| वरेण्यम् | **Selection** (√वृ "choose"): Shannon defined information as the *choice* of one message from a set of possibilities |
| भर्गः | **Signal power**: enough signal-to-noise ratio to "burn through" noise |
| देवस्य | Of the transmitter |
| धीमहि | We **receive, decode and store** |
| धियः | Our priors and internal models (the receiver's state) |
| यः | Which source |
| नः | Ours |
| प्रचोदयात् | **Updates the receiver**: the message has an effect (Level C achieved) |

**Full interpretation.** "Opening the channel, and working at the technical, semantic and effectiveness levels, we receive and decode the selected, high-power signal of that source, so that it may update our internal models and produce an effect."

**Key insight.** In Shannon's theory **information is choice**, and **वरेण्य comes from √वृ, "to choose"**. Vedic transmission also built in **error-correcting codes**: the *pada, krama, jaṭā* and *ghana* recitation modes repeat each word in several permuted orders, which is why oral transmission of the text has been remarkably stable over millennia. The fixed 24-syllable metre adds a further check-sum.

## Interpretation 14: Systems theory

**Padaccheda:** ॐ । भूः । भुवः । स्वः । तत् । सवितुः । वरेण्यम् । भर्गः । देवस्य । धीमहि । धियः । यः । नः । प्रचोदयात् ॥

| पद | Systems value |
|---|---|
| ॐ | The **whole system** as a unit (holon) |
| भूः | **Structure / stocks** |
| भुवः | **Processes / flows** |
| स्वः | **Purpose / goal** (telos, the highest leverage point in Donella Meadows' hierarchy) |
| तत् | The supra-system / environment |
| सवितुः | Of the **driver**, the energy and information input |
| वरेण्यम् | The **reference set-point** that is chosen |
| भर्गः | The **throughput** that keeps the structure going (dissipation) |
| देवस्य | Of the source |
| धीमहि | **Sensing and state estimation**: the feedback path (hold a representation) |
| धियः | **Control logic**: decision rules |
| यः | Which driver |
| नः | Ours |
| प्रचोदयात् | **Actuation**: the control signal (feed-forward path) |

**Full interpretation.** "Across structure, process and purpose, we sense and model the chosen set-point and energising throughput of that driving source, so that it may actuate and improve our decision rules."

**Key insight.** The mantra describes a **closed control loop**: धीमहि (feedback, *we* sense) → प्रचोदयात् (actuation, *he* drives) → धियः (the controller itself is changed). Because what gets impelled is the *control logic* (धी) and not the output, this is **second-order cybernetics**: a system that improves its own regulator. The vyāhṛtis climb to *purpose* (स्वः), the leverage point that Meadows ranks above structure and flows.

## Interpretation 15: Social / education / human-development model

**Padaccheda:** ॐ । भूः । भुवः । स्वः । तत् । सवितुः । वरेण्यम् । भर्गः । देवस्य । धीमहि । धियः । यः । नः । प्रचोदयात् ॥

| पद | Developmental value |
|---|---|
| ॐ | A shared commitment: the common ground of a learning community |
| भूः | **Physical / psychomotor** development (health, livelihood) |
| भुवः | **Affective / social** development (relationships, character) |
| स्वः | **Cognitive / higher** development (knowledge, meaning, self-realisation) |
| तत् | The ideal |
| सवितुः | Of the **teacher, institution or enlightened leader** (consistent with Part 1's ādhibhautika Savitā: guru, parents, king) |
| वरेण्यम् | The **excellence worth choosing** (values) |
| भर्गः | **Tapas**: the discipline that burns away faults |
| देवस्य | Of the giver (*dānāt*: the teacher gives) |
| धीमहि | We **learners together** internalise it (note the plural) |
| धियः | Our **capabilities** (in Amartya Sen's sense: what people are able to do and to be) |
| यः | Which teacher |
| नः | **All of us** (inclusive: "our", never "my") |
| प्रचोदयात् | **Inspires** (intrinsic motivation, as in self-determination theory) |

**Full interpretation.** "Developing in body, in relationships and in mind, we learners together take in the excellence and discipline offered by the giving teacher, so that it may inspire the growth of all our capabilities."

**Key insight.** The Gāyatrī is the **upanayana mantra**, the mantra given at entry into education. What it asks for is significant. It does not ask for wealth or a body of information; it asks for **dhī**, the *capacity* to think, and it asks for it **in the plural (नः)**. In modern terms that is **capability-building as a collective good**, the view of education taken by the human-development approach.

## 7. Synthesis: three findings

### 7.1 The mantra as a schema with free variables

| Grammatical slot | Fixed by Vyākaraṇa | Filled by the lens |
|---|---|---|
| Agent (पुं. प्रथमा: यः = सविता देवः) | an impelling source | star / Hamiltonian / teacher / witness … |
| Object of holding (नपुं. द्वितीया: तत् वरेण्यं भर्गः) | a chosen, transforming energy | exergy / signal / jñānāgni / tapas … |
| Our act (लिङ्, उत्तम बहु.: धीमहि) | collective holding | storage / attention / abiding / learning … |
| His act (लेट्, प्रथम एक.: प्रचोदयात्) | causative forward impulse | entrainment / update / actuation / inspiration … |
| Patient (स्त्री. द्वि. बहु.: धियः) | many capacities | rhythms / networks / models / capabilities … |

### 7.2 Recurring root-level connections

- **√वृ (choose):** वरेण्य ↔ Shannon's information-as-choice ↔ Kaṭha's *vṛṇīte* ↔ basal-ganglia selection ↔ einselection.
- **√सू (impel / generate):** सवितृ ↔ Hamiltonian as "generator" ↔ Yoga's *pratiprasava* (Part 1 §9.3).
- **√भ्रस्ज (cook):** भर्ग ↔ nucleosynthesis ↔ metabolism ↔ tapas ↔ jñānāgni.
- **√धा / √ध्यै (hold / contemplate):** धीमहि ↔ working memory ↔ storage ↔ nididhyāsana.

These links rest on **verifiable dhātus**, not on surface sound-similarity, which is what lifts them above free association.

### 7.3 Śabda → Artha → Tattva

| Level | Gāyatrī |
|---|---|
| **Śabda** | 24 syllables, 14 padas, two non-indicative verbs (लिङ्, लेट्), a neuter energy and a masculine source, a plural patient |
| **Artha** | "Collective holding of a source's chosen transforming energy, which in turn upgrades collective capacity" |
| **Tattva** | Every level at which energy or information from a source is taken in and changes the receiver: stars and planets, oscillators, brains, learners, societies, the self |

## 8. Methodological caveats (for research-level use)

1. **Analogy is not proof.** The scientific lenses (3–8, 13–14) set out *structural homologies*. They show that the mantra's grammatical schema is *compatible* with these models. They do not show that the composers held those models. That is a separate historical claim and needs separate evidence.
2. **Constraint keeps ananta artha honest.** Every row above keeps the case, number, gender and mood of §2 and draws on an attested dhātu or classical authority (§3). An interpretation that breaks the gender crux (for example, making *bharga* the agent of प्रचोदयात्) is **grammatically invalid**, however attractive it may be.
3. **Consistency within a lens.** In each table, once Savitā is given a value, every other word takes its value from the same domain. This is the same rule the author of *Vedārtha-Vijñānam* follows in all 17 bhāṣyas of Part 1.
4. **Empirical references** (Chaisson; Bernardi et al. 2001; Lutz et al. 2008; Shannon–Weaver 1949; Schrödinger 1944; Meadows; Sen) are cited as *context for the analogies*, not as evidence that the mantra encodes them.

> **Conclusion.** Within the limits of Pāṇinian grammar and Yāska's etymology, the Gāyatrī, like *viśvāni deva* in Part 1, works as a **generative template**: one fixed grammatical form that supports many internally consistent, domain-specific readings. That is the precise and defensible sense of **"अनन्ता वै वेदाः"**.
